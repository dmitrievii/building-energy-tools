## Extreme-day presentation refinement
Extreme events and day indicators is the default extreme-day view. The selected daily statistic and the numeric threshold remain plotted when zero events satisfy the minimum continuous duration; no artificial events or intensity points are created. The same shared timeline behavior applies to hourly humidity/wind and wet/dry spells. Missing intervals remain visual gaps. Event-dependent distributions are only offered when qualifying events exist.
