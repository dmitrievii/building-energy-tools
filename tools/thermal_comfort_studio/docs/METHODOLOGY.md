# Methodology

Thermal Comfort Studio calculates whole-body thermal sensation using the PMV/PPD model and supporting heat-balance terms. Inputs include air temperature, mean radiant temperature, relative air speed, relative humidity, metabolic rate, clothing insulation and external work.

The UI reports PMV, PPD, operative temperature and whole-body category screening, and visualises radiation, convection, skin evaporation and respiration. Conduction is shown as `0 W/m²` because it is not a heat-loss term in the ISO 7730 PMV heat-balance model.

The psychrometric and parameter-space charts are explanatory visualisations. PMV/PPD remains the primary whole-body comfort result. Local thermal discomfort is outside the current public UI scope.

This tool is for research, teaching and early design support and does not replace project-specific engineering judgement, regulatory verification, certification or professional design responsibility.
