# IGE P8.4B — Full-Census Historical Wind Reconstruction & Spatial Generalization

**Frozen 2026-10-17 BEFORE original 2022/2025 scientific runs.** Parent P8.4A3 source semantics report `a61d3d951194d5b2e7bfd5d6659326b3ac68e5a6`. Independent research branch; never merge to main/EPW without explicit user approval.

## Rationale and frozen design

P8.3D (2024) drew **92 candidate station files**, not all source-catalog stations, and found an altitude-aware 4-donor selector improved pooled scalar speed MAE from 1.5197 to 1.0428 m/s on six sites. The later P8.3F3 ablation used a **restricted 10-donor training pool**, selecting identical donor identities and giving no evidence about the *full-census* selection factor. P8.4B must not conflate the two.

- Source: original MeteoSwiss SwissMetNet open 2020–2029 hourly historical CSV `fkl010h0` (scalar 1h mean m/s) and `dkl010h0` (meteorological FROM degrees), original station metadata. **EVERY qualifying catalog station identifier** (typically 159) is attempted, not just a target-nearest subset; record each success/failure and original source SHA256. Never publish original hourly observations or provider CSV.
- Frozen *new site holdout targets*: `AIG` (Aigle), `THU` (Thun), `INT` (Interlaken), `RAG` (Bad Ragaz), `ZER` (Zermatt), `VIT` (Vitznau). These IDs are documented as present in original 2024 full candidate coverages, and were not the 2024 P8.3D six targets or 2022/2025 P8.3F3 six target names. They may have served as donors in older research; **not completely virgin geographic domains**.
- Frozen independent chronological tests: **2022** and **2025**, both complete nonleap years (8,760 UTC hours each). No model fitting on either year, no training on target wind values. Target station identities excluded **globally** from all donor pools for all six sites, including each other's target observations.
- Dataset all 159 metadata sites attempted once; a station is qualified **per evaluation year** if >=90% of its expected 8,760 hourly original records are usable under P8.3A wind semantics. First collect all donor weather in RAM; no raw observation upload even to GitHub Actions artifacts. Target >=80% matched full-year clock; all three selectors use strictly common UTC hours; source metadata IDs cannot be substituted by name similarities.
- Frozen methods `distance_idw4`, `altitude_idw4`, `terrain_idw4` exactly as old P8.3D `ranking_score` (`ALT_KM_PER_M=.04`, `TPI_KM_PER_M=.03`, `RUGGED_KM_PER_M=.02`), unchanged P8.3A **physical-distance 1/d² hourly scalar-speed IDW** and vector-direction; P8.3B unit-direction comparator. Actual geographic distances enter weights, ranking scores are never used as weights. DEM Tilezen source sampled for all qualified stations, not only the 92 P8.3D candidates, with original tile SHA summaries.
- Strict region-block stress test: for each target remove **all donors with distance <25 km** and all other target-site IDs; select four donors under both geographic and altitude-ranking. This is a *new 25km holdout stress test*, not independent external country validation or physically simulated inflow. The radius is frozen before results, not tuned by test errors.
- In addition to speed MAE/RMSE/bias and hourly coverage, report direction **resolved fraction on common reference noncalm denominator**, emitted-only circular MAE, strict all-method paired directional MAE, and missing-direction sensitivity (90° diagnostic only, not measured angular error). Do not choose models based on emitted-only direction alone. Report seasonal DJF/MAM/JJA/SON and speed strata (calm <0.5, 0.5–2, 2–5, >=5 m/s) on common hours; same-hour original donors imply **retrospective historical interpolation, NEVER independently generated SSP wind**.
- Predeclared primary score: equal-site mean scalar speed MAE, with annual 2022 and 2025 presented **separately**, and all six individual targets; altitude vs distance paired per site, with 7-calendar-day contiguous-block 400-replicate bootstrap CI, preserving missing gaps and whole-day observation groupings. Site counts and effect heterogeneity matter more than global pooled MAE. Also compare region-block versions on same full-year target hours.
- Predeclared failure flags: source-census not attempted for all metadata sites, catalog unexpectedly <150 valid records, target missing or insufficient data, too few eligible donors (<65 per test year), incomplete coverage (<80%), response/schema mismatch, or DEM source unavailable. **Do not silently limit census** or substitute prior 92 candidates, fake DEM features, or relax thresholds. If operational provider outages occur, preserve precise failures and rerun without changing science.
- Source per-record quality flags in original MeteoSwiss hourly CSV may be absent; do not claim QC-certified observations. Station altitude above mean sea level is **not measured anemometer height**; no roughness, exposure or vertical-profile physics assumed. Historic same-hour scalar speed is distinct from hourly ERA5 instantaneous and GeoSphere vector hourly speeds, as shown P8.4A1–A3.
- Avoid selection leakage: even when availability is evaluated using the holdout calendar year, this is a retrospective complete-data station qualification, not forecast station availability; donors chosen by stationary metadata and source-hour QC presence, never by target observed wind errors. Region-block definition is geographic and fixed.
- Scientific adoption requires repeatable gains across sites AND years, favorable balanced-site CI, no severe worsening of other sites, and no harmful direction coverage effects; even a robust 2022/2025 improvement is not yet a global production approval. `production_epw_ready=false`.
