Project identity

About

Building Energy Tools is an independent research and teaching project for transparent building-physics and building-performance workflows.

Developer

Ivan Dmitriev develops and maintains the project.

Academic affiliation

Institute of Buildings and Energy, Graz University of Technology.

Independence notice: academic affiliation is stated for biographical context only. Building Energy Tools is not an official website, service or product of Graz University of Technology.

Purpose

The project combines calculation tools, teaching material and research documentation. Its design principle is to keep scientific assumptions, provenance and validation evidence visible alongside results.

Engineering scope

Tools on this site support research, education and early design analysis. They do not replace project-specific professional judgement, regulatory verification, certification or design responsibility.
