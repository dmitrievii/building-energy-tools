Methods and development

Research

Research-oriented development at the intersection of building physics, climate-responsive design and computational building-performance methods.

Current themes

Climate-responsive design

Connecting weather data and climate indicators to early design decisions, passive strategies and transparent comparative analysis.

Building energy modelling

Reproducible calculation workflows, model assumptions, standards alignment and validation of energy-performance methods.

Computational methods

Scientific software architecture, automated validation, machine-readable traceability and data-driven building workflows.

Research software as evidence

Building Energy Tools treats software implementation and validation as separate claims. Passing software tests demonstrates the declared tested scope; it does not automatically establish universal scientific, regulatory or project-specific validity.

Validation records, methodology notes and known limitations are therefore published alongside tools rather than hidden behind the interface.
